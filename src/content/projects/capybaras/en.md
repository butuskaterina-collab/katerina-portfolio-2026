      Illustration, HSE School of Design × Khodynka Gallery, Moscow, 2024.

      Khodynka Gallery is a place for an international exhibition, research and education programme beyond disciplinary boundaries.

      For the project every student drew 10 dugongs and 10 capybaras, which were then arranged on the walls and mixed with inscriptions. Some of the dugongs and capybaras wear human clothes, are depressed and do what an average person does: go to work, sleep after work.
