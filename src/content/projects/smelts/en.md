      Public art, HSE School of Design, Saint Petersburg, 2023. A team project: Sofya Melnichenko, Lada Savelyeva, Katerina Butusova and Sofia Petrova, tutor — Ekaterina Filipenko.

      The project marks the 320th anniversary of Saint Petersburg. We chose the fish that many people associate with the city — the smelt. We also took an example from history — verst posts, the old road milestones. Combining the two metaphors gave us the project: 320 smelts shaped as verst posts.

      We cut the smelts out of polystyrene foam sheets. The work went in stages: cutting, priming, sanding, painting white, adding inscriptions and stripes.
