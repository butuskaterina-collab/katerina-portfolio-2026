      A project at the HSE School of Design, 2025. A music video for a song by Splean, neural networks.

      “Daughter of a Samurai” is a poetic story of fatal doom, where Japanese aesthetics become a metaphor for inner unfreedom and fragility, and every gesture a sign of a doomed fate.
  song:
    body: |-
      The song was written by Alexander Vasiliev, the leader of Splean, in memory of Sadako Sasaki, a girl who survived the atomic bombing of Hiroshima at the age of two. Sadako died of leukaemia while trying to fold a thousand paper cranes so that her wish for a carefree life would come true.
  krea:
    body: |-
      The main part shows the heroine’s inner world. For it I generated short fragments in the Krea neural network and processed them in TouchDesigner and After Effects.
