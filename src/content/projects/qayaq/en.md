  about:
    label: About the project
    body: |-
      A project at the HSE School of Design, tutor — Ilya Bannikov-Brumm, 2026. I made the typeface, identity, packaging and brand media, a collaboration with a 3D artist and a pop-up in Yakutsk.

      Value appears in the moment of opening, touching and interacting. The brand is built on a gesture: a trace on the surface of the butter, a fold of parchment, gentle pressure and diffused light. Every gesture leaves a memory.

      QAYAQ creates a space where slowing down becomes a support, and touching the product becomes a way to feel rooted.
  font:
    label: Typeface
    body: |-
      The brand's typographic system is based on early 20th-century Yakut writing — Novgorodov's alphabet, created in 1917 on the basis of the International Phonetic Alphabet.

      The “:” sign for long sounds is designed as a ligature, and the set of ligatures is extended.
  identity:
    label: Identity
    body: |-
      QAYAQ's visual language follows a northern logic of form: centre, axis, symmetry, density, soft light. I interpret Sakha ornamental culture through structure rather than direct quotation.

      Butter records touch. A knife, a spoon, a fold of parchment, the pressure of fingers — every action leaves an imprint. The traces appear unpredictably, yet add up to recognisable forms. The graphics capture the moment when material meets movement.

      Fragments of the logo become signs in their own right: they can be repeated, scaled and assembled into ornaments. They move onto butter stamps, posters and communication materials.
  product:
    label: Product
    body: |-
      The line is based on northern ingredients and local flavour pairings. Each flavour has its own name and graphic sign, and together they form the product system and its cultural context.

      The side of the box leaves space for marking by hand.
  card:
    label: Card
    body: |-
      A card comes with the product and moves it from the category of food into the category of experience. It carries a short formula or a gesture of interaction. It can stay in the kitchen, be kept as an object, become part of a space.
  order:

  carriers:

  collab:
    label: Collaboration
    body: |-
      Together with 3D artist Viktor Nesterov, QAYAQ extended its range with a series of objects about gesture, storage and trace. They continue the ritual of using the product and turn the brand's visual language into material.

      I was responsible for the visual side of the series: concept, packaging, labelling, printed elements and photography. Each object is packed in a linen pouch with a translucent tag.

      Түөскэ (a case for small things), буучугурас (a pin ornament), epoxy resin keychains, оттиск (a butter stamp), көрүҥ (a butter mould), candles and soap.
  popup:
    label: Pop-up
    body: |-
      The pop-up is a temporary space where a person lives through the ritual by way of a simple action. Kvartal Truda, Yakutsk.

      An invitation letter, a tasting layout, an embroidered textile apron for the team and a T-shirt carrying the imprint of the event.
