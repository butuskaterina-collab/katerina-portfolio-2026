texts:
  about:
    label: About the project
    body: |-
      A project at the HSE School of Design, 2025. Motion design.

      Peter Greenaway is a British intellectual filmmaker who combines baroque aesthetics with postmodern play.
  analysis:
    label: 'A: Analysis'
    body: |-
      An analytical video essay designed as a cinematic alphabet. Key letters reveal the director’s system of images and his visual language.
  brand:
    label: 'B: Brand'
    body: |-
      Homo Cinematicus and Avia Poetica are a declaration and a manifesto.

      Homo Cinematicus is a person whose nature and perception are shaped by cinema. Avia Poetica is a poetic flight, a movement of thought and emotion beyond logic and calculation. Together they create a harmony where anatomy intertwines with poetry, science with ecstasy, precision with theatrical passion.
  theatre:
    body: |-
      The central image is an anatomical theatre in plan: a circle with an amphitheatre, the viewer above the object being dissected.
  intro:
    label: 'S: Studio ident'
    body: |-
      The ident presents the film studio as a scientific archive where art exists as “invisible creatures” classified by the laws of Greenaway’s aesthetics.
  metaphor:
    body: |-
      The metaphor: the director is the last exhibit, escaped from his own collection. But even an empty cage carries on the show.
  ophelia:
    body: |-
      An archive film of 16 versions of Ophelia’s death. Each death is like a pressed flower: a fragment of memory, an interpretation or a myth. None of the versions claims to be true.
  archivist:
    body: |-
      An anonymous archivist opens an ancient box with the mysterious name “Herbarium Morbidum”. Inside are 16 glass containers holding Ophelia’s “deaths”.

      Each capsule opens a door to a new world, but all these deaths are fictional, theatrical and often contradict one another.
  magazine:
    body: 'M: Magazine'
  cards:
    body: Business cards for the studio staff
  box:
    body: Gift box
  poster:
    body: Poster for the release of “OPHELIA. HERBARIUM MORBIDUM.”
  catalog:
    body: A catalogue of all of Ophelia’s deaths
  record:
    body: A vinyl record with the film’s soundtrack
