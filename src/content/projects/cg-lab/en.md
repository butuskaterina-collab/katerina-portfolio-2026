      I gathered references, developed the concept and made tech art for 3D vehicle models.

  process:
    body: |-
      Process.

      While searching for the form I deliberately moved away from realism toward simpler, more readable silhouettes. The focus was on solid volumes, a minimum of small details and clear geometry that reads well from a distance.
