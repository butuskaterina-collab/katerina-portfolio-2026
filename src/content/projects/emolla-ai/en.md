      emolla are anti-stress dolls for psychological self-help. The doll helps to relieve stress, and the emotions diary that comes with it helps you understand yourself and be more mindful of your emotional state.
  start:
    label: Getting started
    body: |-
      For the emolla rebrand I used current AI tools: I updated the brand’s visual elements and created new product images, promo materials and a packaging concept.

      Neural networks helped me generate ideas quickly, test different options and speed up the work. The brand became more contemporary and more appealing to its audience while keeping its core concept.

      Tools: KREA, Ideogram and Recraft for image generation, DeepSeek for analysis and writing prompts, What Font Is and Adobe Color for everything else.
  metaphor:
    label: I. Rethinking the metaphor and brand description
    body: |-
      I gave DeepSeek the original project description and asked it to rethink the concept to make it more appealing and contemporary, and to suggest ideas for the visual style.

      In return I got a new metaphor and brand description and several design concepts for further work.
  deepseek:
    label: 'Emolla: a mirror of the soul in textile'
    body: |-
      “Emolla is not just anti-stress dolls but textile companions that reflect your emotions and help you understand yourself better. Each doll becomes a mirror of your inner state, and the emotions diary that comes with it turns your feelings into conscious steps towards harmony. Emolla is self-care through tactility, textures and emotions.”
  identity:
    label: 'II. Identity: logo, colours, typography'
    body: |-
      I wanted the logo to carry the textile metaphor through the character of its lines. I used flowing elements reminiscent of fabric, threads or embroidery and kept the logo concise.

      I made the logo in the Krea neural network: it generated the sign and the wordmark, and I combined them in Photoshop. I also animated the sign in Krea, using the same algorithm as for the sign itself.

      For the colour palette I generated an extra set of moodboard images in Recraft.
  font:
    body: |-
      I uploaded the finished logo to What Font Is and found a similar typeface with a free personal-use licence — Gentium Book Basic Bold by Monotype. I built the palette from the moodboard in Adobe Color.
