    body: |-
      A project at the HSE School of Design, 2025. Motion design, interactive sound installation.

      “it talks” is an interactive multimedia installation: unspoken emotions turn into a dynamic space on the walls.

      The idea is to create an immersive environment where suppressed emotions take visual form and the viewer enters a dialogue with their inner world and makes sense of the unspoken.

      The visuals are abstract, so each viewer reads them in their own way and lives through their own emotions. Their interaction becomes a dialogue that helps to sort through inner experiences.
