  about:
    label: About the project
    body: |-
      A team project of the HSE School of Design for Afisha Picnic, 2026. I co-led the project and was its art director: I organised the development of the concept and stencils, coordinated the team, corrected layouts, drew up the budget, shot photos and video, worked with participants on site and put together the portfolio.

      For Afisha Picnic × School of Design our team came up with a postcard workshop. Festival guests assembled their own compositions from ready-made stencils and applied them with acrylic paint using sponges and brushes.

      We developed the visual direction, graphic elements and stencils, prepared the materials and worked on site.
  graphics:
    label: Graphics
    body: |-
      The graphics draw on images tied to the festival atmosphere: rides, circus, characters, toys, decorative shapes and short inscriptions. We gathered them into a library of elements that could be combined on a postcard.
  lettering:
    label: Lettering
    body: |-
      Short phrases became graphic elements in their own right. We wrote them in different hands to vary the rhythm of a composition and pair the words with images.
  prep:
    label: Preparation
    body: |-
      From 13:00 to 14:00 the team set up the space, laid out materials and made test prints. The workshop ran from 14:00 to 21:00.
  workshop:
    label: Workshop and results
    body: |-
      Guests chose stencils, applied paint and assembled their postcards on the spot. More than 100 people took part.
